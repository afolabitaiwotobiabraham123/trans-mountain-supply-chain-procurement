# Project Reports

This folder contains the written reports documenting the findings, analysis results, forecasting outcomes, and business insights generated throughout the Trans Mountain Supply Chain, Procurement & Forecasting Analytics project.

## Reports

### data_quality_report.md
Documents the results of data quality assessment, including missing values, duplicate records, invalid values, inconsistent categories, and other identified data issues.

### procurement_analysis.md
Presents findings from procurement spending, purchasing activities, purchase order volumes, and procurement performance.

### supplier_performance_analysis.md
Documents supplier delivery performance, reliability, lead-time variations, and identified supplier-related issues.

### forecasting_analysis.md
Presents forecasting methods, model results, predicted procurement expenditure, estimated material demand, supplier lead-time predictions where supported, and forecast evaluation metrics.

### business_insights.md
Summarizes key findings, procurement inefficiencies, supplier performance issues, future supply chain risks, and evidence-based recommendations.

## Reporting Approach

Each report will document:

- Objective of the analysis.
- Data and methods used.
- Findings and observations.
- KPI calculations, where applicable.
- Forecasting results and limitations, where applicable.
- Business implications.
- Evidence-based recommendations.

**Note:** Reports will be completed after the relevant datasets have been collected, assessed, and analyzed. No findings or forecasts will be assumed in advance.
